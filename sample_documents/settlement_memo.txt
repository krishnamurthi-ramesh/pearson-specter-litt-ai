This memo provides a status update on the settlement negotiations in
Donovan v. Meridian Healthcare Systems, Case No. 2023-CV-05891 (S.D.N.Y.).

BACKGROUND

Our client, Meridian Healthcare Systems ("Meridian"), is a defendant in a
class action lawsuit filed by former employee Angela Donovan on behalf of
approximately 2,300 current and former employees. The Complaint, filed on
September 12, 2023, alleges violations of the Fair Labor Standards Act
("FLSA") and New York Labor Law, specifically:

(1) Failure to pay overtime compensation for hours worked in excess of 40
    hours per week;
(2) Improper classification of registered nurses as exempt employees;
(3) Failure to provide required meal and rest periods.

The putative class includes all non-supervisory registered nurses employed
by Meridian at its five New York facilities between January 1, 2020 and
the present.

SETTLEMENT ANALYSIS

Opposing counsel, Rachel Zane of Zane & Associates, has proposed a global
settlement of $8,500,000.00. Their demand is broken down as follows:

- Back wages:          $5,100,000.00
- Liquidated damages:  $2,400,000.00
- Attorney's fees:       $850,000.00
- Named plaintiff award: $150,000.00

Our analysis suggests the following exposure:

BEST CASE (if we prevail on exemption defense):
- Total liability: $0
- Probability: 25%

MODERATE CASE (partial class, limited damages period):
- Total liability: $3,200,000 - $4,800,000
- Probability: 45%

WORST CASE (full class certification, all claims):
- Total liability: $12,000,000 - $15,000,000
- Probability: 30%

Expected value: approximately $5,400,000.00

RECOMMENDATION

Given the litigation risk profile, I recommend we counter-offer at
$4,750,000.00, structured as follows:

- Back wages fund:      $2,850,000.00
- Liquidated damages:   $1,200,000.00
- Attorney's fees:        $550,000.00
- Named plaintiff award:  $100,000.00
- Administrative costs:    $50,000.00

This counter-offer represents approximately 55.8% of opposing counsel's
demand and is within the range of our moderate-case exposure estimate.

The deadline for responding to the settlement proposal is March 20, 2024.

KEY RISKS

1. Judge Thompson has been favorable to FLSA plaintiffs in recent rulings
2. Similar cases in this district have resulted in class certification
   rates of approximately 72%
3. Meridian's internal audit (conducted by Deloitte in Q4 2023) identified
   "systemic issues" with time recording for nursing staff — this document
   could be highly damaging if produced in discovery
