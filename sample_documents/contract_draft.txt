AGREEMENT OF PURCHASE AND SALE

This Agreement of Purchase and Sale ("Agreement") is entered into as of
January 15, 2024, by and between:

Plaintiff: Greenfield Properties LLC, a Delaware limited liability company
("Seller"), with offices at 1234 Commerce Drive, Suite 500, Wilmington, DE 19801

AND

Defendant: Harborview Capital Partners Inc., a New York corporation
("Buyer"), with offices at 789 Wall Street, 22nd Floor, New York, NY 10005

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Seller is the owner of certain real property located at 456 Elm
Street, Hartford, CT 06103, legally described in Exhibit A attached hereto
(the "Property");

WHEREAS, Buyer desires to purchase the Property from Seller, and Seller
desires to sell the Property to Buyer, upon the terms and conditions set
forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements
contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt
and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

SECTION 1: PURCHASE PRICE AND PAYMENT

1.1 Purchase Price. The total purchase price for the Property shall be
Three Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($3,500,000.00) (the "Purchase
Price").

1.2 Earnest Money Deposit. Within five (5) business days after the
Effective Date, Buyer shall deposit the sum of One Hundred Seventy-Five
Thousand Dollars ($175,000.00) (the "Deposit") with First National
Escrow Company (the "Escrow Agent").

1.3 Balance at Closing. The balance of the Purchase Price, subject to
adjustments and prorations as provided herein, shall be paid by Buyer to
Seller at Closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

SECTION 2: DUE DILIGENCE PERIOD

2.1 Inspection Period. Buyer shall have a period of forty-five (45) days
from the Effective Date (the "Due Diligence Period") within which to
conduct its inspections and investigations of the Property.

2.2 Environmental Assessment. Buyer may, at Buyer's sole cost and expense,
obtain a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment of the Property. If the
Phase I Assessment reveals the presence or likely presence of any
Recognized Environmental Conditions, Buyer may terminate this Agreement
by written notice to Seller prior to the expiration of the Due Diligence
Period.

2.3 Title Examination. Within fifteen (15) days after the Effective Date,
Seller shall provide Buyer with a commitment for title insurance issued
by a nationally recognized title company. Buyer shall have until the
expiration of the Due Diligence Period to review and approve the title
commitment.

SECTION 3: REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

3.1 Seller's Representations. Seller represents and warrants to Buyer
that:
(a) Seller has full power and authority to execute this Agreement and
    to perform its obligations hereunder.
(b) There are no pending or threatened legal proceedings affecting
    the Property.
(c) To Seller's knowledge, the Property complies with all applicable
    zoning ordinances, building codes, and environmental regulations.
(d) There are no leases, tenancies, or occupancy agreements affecting
    the Property except as disclosed in Schedule 3.1(d).

3.2 Buyer's Representations. Buyer represents and warrants to Seller
that:
(a) Buyer has sufficient funds or financing commitments to complete
    the purchase of the Property.
(b) Buyer has full power and authority to execute this Agreement.

SECTION 4: CLOSING

4.1 Closing Date. The closing of the purchase and sale contemplated
hereby (the "Closing") shall take place on or before March 15, 2024,
at the offices of the Escrow Agent, unless the parties mutually agree
in writing to an extension.

4.2 Closing Costs. Seller shall pay for the title insurance premium,
transfer taxes, and Seller's attorney fees. Buyer shall pay for
recording fees, lender's title insurance, and Buyer's attorney fees.

SECTION 5: DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

5.1 If Buyer defaults under this Agreement, Seller shall be entitled
to retain the Deposit as liquidated damages, and this Agreement shall
thereupon be terminated.

5.2 If Seller defaults under this Agreement, Buyer shall be entitled
to seek specific performance or, at Buyer's election, to terminate
this Agreement and receive a full refund of the Deposit.

SECTION 6: MISCELLANEOUS

6.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed
in accordance with the laws of the State of Connecticut.

6.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement
between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

6.3 Notices. All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the
addresses set forth above.
